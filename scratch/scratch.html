My Personal Ranking of the Six Films

  1. Episode III – Revenge of the Sith
    • Order 66 is CRAZY
    • Best lightsaber battles in the saga
    • Anakin, the chosen one, falling to the dark side
  2. Episode I – The Phantom Menace
    • Duel of the Fates is so good
    • Young Anakin podracing is unbeatable
    • Obi-Wan and Qui-Gon are the dream team
  3. Episode II – Attack of the Clones
    • Kamino and the clone army reveal
    • Makes you want to watch the Clone Wars Series
    • Introduction of one of the best love stories, Anakin and Padme
  4. Episode V – The Empire Strikes Back
  5. Episode IV – A New Hope
  6. Episode VI – Return of the Jedi
